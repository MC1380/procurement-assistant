## 3. Operating Principle

An RTD measures temperature through the change in electrical resistance
of its sensing element.

A Pt100 is a platinum resistance temperature detector with a nominal
resistance of 100 ohms at 0°C.

As temperature changes, the resistance of the platinum element changes.
A suitable measurement instrument measures this resistance and converts
it into a temperature value.

The Pt100 nominal resistance characteristic does not, by itself,
establish the accuracy or operating limits of this particular assembly.

## 4. Three-Wire Configuration

A three-wire RTD configuration uses three conductors to help compensate
for the resistance of the connecting wires.

This compensation is useful because lead-wire resistance can introduce
measurement error.

The effectiveness of compensation depends on the measurement instrument
and the electrical characteristics of the wiring.

A three-wire configuration does not guarantee a specific measurement
accuracy without additional technical information.
